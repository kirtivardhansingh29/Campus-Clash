# 🎓 Campus Clash

**The college fighting game — attendance kam, attitude full!** 🥊

Campus Clash is a light-hearted, slapstick browser fighting game set in a fictional college. Pick your fighter — the topper, the backbencher, the warden, the canteen bhaiya, even the placement cell boss — choose an arena, and settle campus scores the funny way. No gore, only gags.

## 🎮 Play it

- **Live (Muse):** https://muse.ai/s/campus-clash-xdn6vpwmxjxvkxz/ploNDeK3c5Nxuvn8JenoXoGWJ_wmN2w7_5gqVee1kmc
- Or just open `index.html` from this repo in any modern browser — it's a single self-contained file, no server or build step needed.

## 🏟️ Arenas

Canteen · Library · Hostel · Auditorium · Classroom · Main Gate · Lab · Placement Cell Office — each with its own crowd, news ticker and music flavour.

## 🕹️ Modes

- **VS CPU** — quick fight against the computer
- **2 Player** — same keyboard, dost ke saath
- **Semester Mode** — an 8-stage campaign ending at the Placement Cell boss fight
- **Orientation Day** — practice mode
- **🌐 Online Dhamaka** — create a room, share the 5-letter code, and fight a friend over the internet (peer-to-peer)

## ⌨️ Controls

**Player 1:** `A`/`D` move · `W` jump · `S` crouch · hold back to block · `J` punch · `K` kick · `L` grab · `U` style move · `SPACE` special

**Player 2:** Arrow keys move · `,` punch · `.` kick · `/` grab · `M` style move · `Right Shift` special

Fill your **Attendance Meter** to unlock your special. Rounds are 60 seconds, best of 3 — and yes, there is a marksheet at the end.

## 🛠️ Tech

- Single-file HTML — Three.js for the 3D arenas, photo-cutout fighters in 2.5D
- 100% procedural WebAudio sound & music (no audio files)
- Online multiplayer via WebRTC (PeerJS) — host-authoritative, room codes, no sign-up
- Progress saved in `localStorage`

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

Campus Clash is a work of fiction. The college, characters and events are imaginary and made purely for fun — any resemblance to your own campus life is... probably accurate, but coincidental. 😉
